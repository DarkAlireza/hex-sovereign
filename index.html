One-Paragraph Rules

Compete over 12 rounds on a 19-hex island to be first to 20 Crowns (or lead at Round 12). Each turn gives 2 Action Points (AP):
  • March (1 AP): Move units - 1 into an adjacent hex. Attacker adds +1 Forge bonus; Defender adds +2 Bastion shield. Conquering the center Crown Spire (0,0) grants +3 Crowns (+2/round).
  • Harvest (1 AP): Gain 2 + 2×Crystal Gold (+1 Crown if holding Spire).
  • Recruit (1 AP, 2G): Add +2 units to a controlled hex (max 9).
  • Bastion (1 AP, 3G): Fortify a hex (+2 Defense, +1 Crown now & per round).
  • Arcane Strike (1 AP, 3G): Deal -2 units & shatter Bastion on an adjacent enemy hex.
